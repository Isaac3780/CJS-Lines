A Word To Ponder

Brethren, Are We Truly Living Out Our Calling?

A reflective call to examine whether our faith has become routine. We fill services and buildings, yet the lost outside remain unreached. God's heart is for the lost — like the shepherd who leaves the ninety-nine for the one. We are saved to serve, loved to love, and called to make disciples, not just attendees. May we step beyond the four walls to reach the hurting, forgotten, and lost.
